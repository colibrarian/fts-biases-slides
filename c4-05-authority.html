Chapter 4 · Persuasion

Appeal to authority

"It is true because a famous person said so", especially outside their field.

How it works

Trusting experts is a useful shortcut. The trick borrows trust from a person who is not an expert on this topic.

Example

A football player advertises a medicine. A doctor's white coat in an ad for a juice.
