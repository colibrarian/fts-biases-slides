Chapter 2 · Filling the gaps

One vivid case

One living story outweighs the statistics.

How it works

We feel for one named person far more than for thousands in a table. Psychologists call it the identifiable victim effect.

Example

"My grandfather smoked all his life and lived to 90, so smoking can't be that bad."

Honest use: a story plus a number. The story makes people care, the number shows how common it is.

Small, D., Loewenstein, G. & Slovic, P. (2007). Sympathy and callousness.
