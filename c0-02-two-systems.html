Chapter 0 · What is a bias

Two ways of thinking

System 1

Fast

Automatic, effortless, always on. Recognises a face, reads the mood of a room, finishes the sentence for you. Makes most of our decisions.

System 2

Slow

Deliberate, effortful, lazy. Checks the maths, weighs the arguments, notices a trick. Only switches on when it has to.

Most cognitive biases are born in System 1 and slip past System 2 unnoticed.
