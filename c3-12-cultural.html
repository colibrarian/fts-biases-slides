Cultural bias and us vs them

We take the norms of our own culture as the norm, and we are softer on our own group.

How it works

Our habits feel neutral because we never see them from outside. Other habits look like a deviation from "normal".

Example

"They greet people strangely" instead of "they greet people differently".

Tajfel, H. & Turner, J. (1979). An integrative theory of intergroup conflict.
