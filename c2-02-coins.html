Thirty flips, twice

Step 1

Invent

Write a "random" row of 30 flips: H or T. Do not use a coin.

Step 2

Flip

Now flip a real coin 30 times and write down every result.

Step 3

Count

In each row, find the longest streak of the same side in a row.
