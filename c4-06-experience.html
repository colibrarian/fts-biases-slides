Chapter 4 · Persuasion

Appeal to personal experience

A personal story is presented as proof of a general rule.

How it works

It uses our belief in one vivid case. The story is real, but it stands in for evidence it cannot give.

Example

"I tried this diet and lost 10 kg, so it works for everyone."

Plays on: one vivid case (ch. 2).
