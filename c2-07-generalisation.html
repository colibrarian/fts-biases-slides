Chapter 2 · Filling the gaps

Hasty generalisation

A conclusion about everyone, drawn from a few cases.

How it works

A small sample feels like enough when it fits a story we already have. The cases we did not see do not argue back.

Example

"Two participants from that country were late, so they are all unreliable."

Plenary question: where do we judge a whole group by one teenager?
