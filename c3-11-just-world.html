Just-world belief

The feeling that everyone gets what they deserve.

How it works

If the world is fair, bad outcomes must be earned. That makes us feel safe, and it makes us blame the person who lost.

Example

"She brought it on herself." "Poor people just don't work hard enough." The jury: "that team didn't try".

Lerner, M. J. (1980). The Belief in a Just World.
