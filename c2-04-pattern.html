Illusion of pattern

We see order and meaning in random coincidences.

How it works

The brain is built to find patterns, and missing a real one used to be more dangerous than seeing a fake one. So it errs on the side of seeing too many.

Example

A "lucky" shirt for matches. Three bad days in a row and "this week is cursed". A cluster of cases on a map that is pure chance.
