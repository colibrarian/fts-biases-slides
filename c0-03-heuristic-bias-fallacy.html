Three words for the week

Heuristic

A shortcut

A rule of thumb the brain uses to decide fast. Usually it works: "if everyone is running, run".

→
Cognitive bias

A predictable error

Where the shortcut systematically leads us wrong. Not random: we all fail the same way.

→
Logical fallacy

A flawed argument

An error in reasoning. Sometimes a mistake, sometimes a tool used on purpose to exploit our biases.
