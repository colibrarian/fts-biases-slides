The framing effect

600 people are at risk. Same outcomes, two ways of saying them.

Half A · saving lives

Programme 1: 200 people will be saved.
Programme 2: 1/3 chance all 600 saved, 2/3 chance nobody.

72% pick 1
Half B · losing lives

Programme 1: 400 people will die.
Programme 2: 1/3 chance nobody dies, 2/3 chance all 600 die.

78% pick 2

"90% survive" and "10% die" are the same fact. The frame picks the decision.

Tversky, A. & Kahneman, D. (1981). The framing of decisions and the psychology of choice.
