Teleology

The belief that everything happens for a purpose.

How it works

We explain events by their goal instead of their cause. It feels like an explanation, but it adds an intention nobody had.

Example

"It rains so that plants can grow." "Giraffes have long necks to reach the leaves." "This happened to me so that I would learn something."

Kelemen, D. (1999). Why are rocks pointy? Children's preference for teleological explanations.
