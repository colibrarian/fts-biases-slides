Availability heuristic

The easier an example comes to mind, the more common and likely it feels.

How it works

We do not count cases, we check how quickly one comes to mind. Vivid, recent and scary events come to mind fastest.

Example

Sharks kill around 10 people a year. Mosquito-borne diseases kill over 700,000. Guess which one gets the films.
