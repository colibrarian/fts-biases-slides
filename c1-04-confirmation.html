Confirmation bias

We look for, notice and remember what supports what we already believe.

How it works

At station 1 most people test triples like 8, 10, 12 that fit their guess. Few try 1, 2, 3 or 6, 4, 2, the tests that could prove them wrong. The rule was just any three increasing numbers.

Example

Your feed shows you posts you agree with, so it feels like "everyone" thinks the same. Each like teaches the feed to filter harder.

Wason, P. C. (1960). On the failure to eliminate hypotheses in a conceptual task.
