Fundamental attribution error

We explain other people's behaviour by their character, and our own by circumstances.

How it works

We see the other person, but not their morning. We see our own morning, so the circumstances are obvious to us.

Example

"He was late because he is irresponsible." "I was late because of the bus."

Ross, L. (1977). The intuitive psychologist and his shortcomings.
