Anchoring

The first number we hear becomes the starting point, even when it is obviously random.

How it works

One half was asked "Did Gandhi die before or after age 9?", the other "before or after age 140?". Both anchors are absurd, yet the average guesses split apart. He was 78.

Example

"Was €200, now €99." The crossed-out price is the anchor that makes €99 feel cheap.

Strack, F. & Mussweiler, T. (1997). Explaining the enigmatic anchoring effect.
