Negativity bias

Bad weighs more than good.

How it works

One negative episode outweighs several positive ones. Threats used to matter more for survival than pleasures.

Example

Out of ten reviews, the one critical review is the one you remember.

Baumeister, R. et al. (2001). Bad is stronger than good.
