Chapter 1 · Filter · Experiment

Three stations, ten minutes each

Station 1

Guess the rule

The trainer has a rule. "2, 4, 6" follows it. Suggest your own triples, hear yes or no, then name the rule.

Station 2

Estimate

Read the question on your card and write down a number. Do not show it to the other half.

Station 3

Choose

A city must pick one of two programmes against a new disease. Read your card and choose.
