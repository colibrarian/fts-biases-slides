Chapter 0 · Where this comes from

Two psychologists, one big idea

1934–2024

Daniel Kahneman

Psychologist. Nobel Memorial Prize in Economics in 2002 for showing how people really decide under uncertainty. Wrote Thinking, Fast and Slow (2011).

1937–1996

Amos Tversky

His research partner. Together they started the study of heuristics and biases in the 1970s. Most of the classic experiments this week are theirs.

Tversky, A. & Kahneman, D. (1974). Judgment under uncertainty: heuristics and biases. Science.
